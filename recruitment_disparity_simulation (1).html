Mathematical Model & Literature Base

1. The Resume Sieve (Kline, Rose, & Walters 2021; Bertrand & Mullainathan 2004)

Correspondence audits submitting identical CVs across Fortune 500 portals document a 20% to 50% discount in initial recruiter callbacks for resumes bearing distinctive Black names or identity cues. This automated or human screen acts before any substantive interview takes place.

2. The Referral Advantage & Social Capital Segregation

Referrals represent ~40% of professional hires and bypass cold screener filtration with 3x higher callback rates. Because senior management tiers remain demographically homogenous, referral networks exhibit intense demographic homophily, heavily disadvantaging Black women.

3. The Qualification Myth (BLS & Economic Policy Institute)

Data confirms that holding a Master's or Doctoral credential does not shield Black women from hiring disparities. In empirical labor datasets, Black college graduates consistently face unemployment and underemployment rates higher than white peers with only high school diplomas or associate credentials.

4. Downstream Evaluation Homophily (McKinsey & LeanIn)

During unstructured onsite interviews, subjective criteria ("presence", "cultural fit") introduce unstandardized risk-aversion, compounding cumulative attrition into final offer stages.
